Definition used (fixed, disclosed, not guessed per-event): a resting order ≥ $15,000 that disappears from the top 20 book levels within ~2 seconds, with no trade of comparable size printing at that price in the same window. This pattern is consistent with spoofing but has innocent explanations too (a trader simply cancelling, or a fast partial fill this feed didn't catch) — it is a raw pattern flag, not a confirmed detection or a trade signal.
