About Dark Crypto

This site is run by Uzair Ali, creator of the Dark Crypto YouTube channel. It exists as one official place for the channel's audience to find every real link — YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp, and TikTok — along with live crypto market data and daily written updates.

The goal is simple: give viewers a single, trustworthy hub instead of chasing scattered or fake links across platforms. Everything linked from this site is verified and controlled directly by Uzair Ali — no third parties, no impersonators.

The live market widgets on this site (prices, Fear & Greed Index, liquidations, volume data) pull directly from public data providers such as CoinGecko, Binance, DeFiLlama, and Coinbase. This site does not provide financial advice — see the Terms & Disclaimer page for details.
