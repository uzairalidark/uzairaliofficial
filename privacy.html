Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dark Crypto website ("this site") handles data when you visit.

1. Information We Collect

This site does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to browse it. We do not operate our own database of visitor personal data.

2. Cookies & Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. This is known as personalized advertising.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page.

3. Third-Party Data Providers

This site displays live market data pulled directly by your browser from third-party public APIs, including CoinGecko, Binance, Coinbase, DeFiLlama, and Alternative.me. These requests are made client-side to fetch public market data only; no personal information is sent to these providers by this site.

4. GDPR & CCPA

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have rights regarding data collected about you, including the right to opt out of personalized advertising as described above. This site relies on Google AdSense's own compliance mechanisms for these regions.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
